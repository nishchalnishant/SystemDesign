# Chapter 5 — Why Should I Learn Patterns?

The truth is that you **might manage to work as a programmer for many years without knowing
about a single pattern**. A lot of people do just that. Even in that case, though, you might
be implementing some patterns without even knowing it.

So why spend time learning them? Two reasons:

### 1. A toolkit of tried and tested solutions

Design patterns are a toolkit of tried and tested solutions to common problems in software
design. **Even if you never encounter these problems**, knowing patterns is still useful
because it teaches you **how to solve all sorts of problems using principles of
object-oriented design**.

### 2. A common language

Design patterns define a common language that you and your teammates can use to communicate
more efficiently. You can say, *"Oh, just use a Singleton for that,"* and everyone will
understand the idea behind your suggestion. No need to explain what a singleton is if you
know the pattern and its name.
